### Step 1: Change Content (5 minutes)
Edit `home-beta.component.html`:
- Update headings
- Change descriptions
- Add contact info
- Update links

### Step 2: Add Images (10 minutes)
Edit `home-beta.component.scss`:

- Add your company photos
- Update logo images

}
