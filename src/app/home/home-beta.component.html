OUR TOWN. OUR PRIDE.

Our town, our people, all in one place!

CDM360 is created to make life easier for the people of Chidambaram. Our aim is to bring our local community together and help people find the services, businesses, jobs, educational resources, and useful information they need in one place.

Through CDM360, you can discover local service providers, explore nearby businesses, find job opportunities, learn about government exams, share community updates, and help local businesses reach more people.

Our mission is simple: Help our people, support our local businesses, and build a stronger community together.
